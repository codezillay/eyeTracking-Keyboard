GazeSpeak

A hands-free, eye-controlled communication keyboard.

  1. Prop up your phone/tablet at eye level, about 30–40 cm away.
  2. Make sure your face is well lit and the front camera can see both eyes.
  3. Tap Start Eye Tracking and allow camera access.
  4. Complete the short calibration by looking steadily at each dot.
  5. Type by looking at a key and holding your gaze until it fills.

No video ever leaves your device — all eye tracking runs locally in your browser.

How to use GazeSpeak

  • Setup: Mount the device steadily at eye level, 30–40cm from the face, in good even lighting.
  • Calibration: Look only with your eyes (avoid head movement) at each dot until it disappears. Do this in the same position you will use for typing.
  • Typing: Look at a key — a ring will fill around it. When full, the key is typed. Look away and back to repeat a key.
  • Word suggestions: Predicted words appear above the keyboard — look at one to insert it instantly.
  • Quick Phrases: Switch tabs to access common needs like "Call nurse" or "I am in pain" with a single dwell.
  • Losing accuracy? Open Settings → Recalibrate anytime, especially after moving the device or changing lighting.
  • No camera? Use Pointer Mode to control the keyboard with a mouse, touch, switch device, or head-mouse instead.
